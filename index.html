The challenge

Good decisions need connected information.

Rice mills generate valuable information at every stage, but production, quality, raw material and maintenance records are often separated across spreadsheets, paper records or disconnected systems.

That makes it harder to understand why losses happen, react to equipment failures and protect the quality of the final product.

◌

Scattered data

Important information is difficult to find and compare.

↯

Unexpected downtime

Reactive maintenance can interrupt production and increase costs.

◒

Hidden losses

Merma, broken rice and quality deviations are detected too late.
